# Data Retention Policy

## Overview

This policy defines how long data is stored to meet business, legal, and compliance requirements.

## Standard Retention Periods

- Production data: Based on business requirements
- Backup data: 30–365 days
- Logs: 90–365 days
- Audit records: 1–7 years
