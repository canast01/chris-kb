# Linux Lifecycle

RHEL follows a 10-year lifecycle: RHEL 8 reaches end of maintenance in 2029, RHEL 9 in 2032. Ubuntu LTS releases receive 5 years of standard support (extendable to 10 years with Ubuntu Pro/ESM). EOL tracking is maintained in the CMDB with automated alerts raised 12 months before support expiry. Kernel updates are applied monthly during patching windows; major OS migrations are planned 18 months in advance and follow a build-new/decommission-old approach.

| OS | Version | EOM/EOL |
|---|---|---|
| RHEL | 8 | May 2029 |
| RHEL | 9 | May 2032 |
| Ubuntu LTS | 22.04 | Apr 2027 (std) / Apr 2032 (ESM) |
| Ubuntu LTS | 24.04 | Apr 2029 (std) / Apr 2034 (ESM) |
