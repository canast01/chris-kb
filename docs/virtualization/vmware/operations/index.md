## Overview

Operations for the VMware platform are organized per component. Each product (vCenter, ESXi, vSAN, NSX) has its own Operations page with daily checks, health checks, change readiness checklists, and incident triage steps.

For cross-platform health check templates and troubleshooting guides, see the sections above.
